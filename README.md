# Fisher-Price Rolex 

A client asked me to rebuild the exterior of a replica Rolex as if Fisher-Price had designed it.

## The Project

The client supplied a replica watch that I disassembled while retaining the internal watch components.

I redesigned the external structure from scratch, including the case, bracelet, links, backing, and surrounding geometry, then manufactured the final design using multicolor 3D printing.

## Design Constraints

The new exterior had to:

- Fit around the existing internal watch hardware
- Remain wearable and comfortable
- Preserve functional bracelet articulation
- Maintain consistent fit between printed components
- Have a screw-on back for easy internal component access
- Have a proper press-fitted replica crystal into the 3d print
- Look intentional rather than like a rough prototype
- Support multicolor additive manufacturing

The design required multiple iterations, particularly around the bracelet links, mating interfaces, the crystal press fit, and fit around the original watch internals.

The bracelet became one of the most demanding parts of the project because small tolerance changes had a large effect on articulation, looseness, and assembly.

- Dimensional accuracy

## Manufacturing

The final watch was produced using multicolor FDM 3D printing on a Bambu Lab X1C with AMS. The filaments used were:
- Red/blue/yellow Bambu Lab PLA+

Some of the biggest issues were adhesion and getting a working printer/ams, as the printer/s were in the community engineering center at my school, and the beds/toolhead/ams/hotend were not well maintained or taken care of.

The printing process required attention to orientation, support strategy, fit, surface finish, and dimensional behavior across multiple interacting parts.

## Final Result

The completed piece retained the original internal watch components while replacing the exterior with a fully redesigned, wearable, multicolor printed assembly.
